Membership Options

Explore the membership options available at FitLine.

We offer a variety of membership plans to suit your fitness needs and budget.

Note: All membership options include access to our child care facilities.

Basic Membership

Perfect for those just starting their fitness journey. This includes access to one location, basic equipment, and standard class offerings.

Price: $29.99/month

Premium Membership

Ideal for those who want more personalized attention and access to exclusive facilities. This plan offers enhanced amenities such as sauna, red light therapy, cold tub, and priority booking on classes available. Premium members also receive exclusive discounts on merchandise and supplements.

Price: $49.99/month

VIP Membership

For the most dedicated members, offering the highest level of service and access. This plan includes all premium benefits plus exclusive events and personalized training sessions from our expert trainers. VIP members also have access to a dedicated account manager and personalized locker availability.

Price: $79.99/month

Note

All memberships are subject to a one-time initiation fee of $50. Memberships can be canceled at any time with a 30-day notice. For more information, please contact our support team.
